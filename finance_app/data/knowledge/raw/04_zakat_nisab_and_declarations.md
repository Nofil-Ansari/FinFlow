# Zakat & Ushr Regulations, Nisab Benchmarks & Exemption Forms

## Legal Framework: Zakat and Ushr Ordinance 1980
Under the Zakat and Ushr Ordinance 1980, compulsory deduction of Zakat is carried out annually on the 1st of Ramadan across all scheduled commercial banks and designated financial institutions on qualifying savings, PLS, and fixed deposit accounts.

---

## Compulsory Bank Deduction Mechanism

| Parameter | Regulatory Detail |
|---|---|
| **Deduction Rate** | **2.5%** flat on qualifying balances |
| **Deduction Date** | 1st of Ramadan (Valuation Date announced by Ministry of Religious Affairs) |
| **Bank Holiday** | Banks remain closed for public transactions on deduction day to process audits |
| **Accounts Subject to Deduction** | Savings Accounts, Notice Deposit Accounts, Fixed Deposit Receipts, Term Deposit Receipts (TDRs), National Savings Scheme accounts |
| **Accounts Exempt from Compulsory Deduction** | Current Accounts (Checking), Foreign Currency Accounts, Non-Muslim customer accounts, accounts with valid CZ-50 affidavit on file |

---

## Nisab Thresholds (Silver vs Gold)

Under Shariah and official declarations:
1. **Official State Bank Nisab (Silver Basis)**:
   - For statutory bank deductions, the Ministry of Religious Affairs calculates the Nisab based on **52.5 tolas of silver** (~612.36 grams) at the prevailing wholesale market price just prior to Ramadan.
   - Example 2024–2025 range: Approximately PKR 135,000 to PKR 150,000 depending on commodity price fluctuations. Accounts holding balances equal to or greater than this announced threshold on 1st Ramadan are subject to automatic 2.5% deduction.
2. **Wealth Calculation (Gold Basis)**:
   - Gold standard is **7.5 tolas of gold** (~87.48 grams). For personal asset wealth evaluations (cash, gold jewelry, tradable inventory), scholars generally refer to the silver threshold as the safer hurdle for compulsory duty.

## Zakat Exemption Procedure (Declaration Form CZ-50)
Individuals who belong to recognized fiqh jurisprudence that exempts them from state-level collection, or who wish to distribute their Zakat personally directly to verified beneficiaries, can seek full exemption from compulsory bank deductions:
- **Declaration Document**: **Form CZ-50** (Zakat Exemption Declaration / Affidavit on non-judicial stamp paper, attested by a Notary Public / Oath Commissioner).
- **Submission Timeline**: Must be submitted to the customer's bank branch **at least 30 days prior** to 1st of Ramadan.
- **Account Tagging**: Once recorded in the Core Banking System (CBS), the account is flagged as "Zakat Exempt", preventing any automated deduction on Ramadan 1.
