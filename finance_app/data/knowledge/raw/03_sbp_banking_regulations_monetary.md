# State Bank of Pakistan (SBP) Banking & Monetary Regulations

## Overview & Monetary Policy Framework
The State Bank of Pakistan regulates scheduled commercial banks, microfinance banks, and payment system operators. The Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) sets the central Policy Rate (reverse repo rate), which acts as the benchmark for commercial deposit and lending rates across the country.

---

## Minimum Savings Rate Rule (SBP Floor on PLS Savings)
Under SBP BSD Circulars, all commercial banks offering conventional Profit and Loss Sharing (PLS) savings accounts are mandated to pay a minimum profit rate linked directly to the SBP Policy Rate:
- **Floor Rule**: Commercial bank PLS savings deposit rate must be at least **Policy Rate minus 150 basis points (1.5%)**.
- **Calculation Basis**: Profit must be calculated on the **average daily balance** (or minimum monthly balance as mandated for specific legacy tiers) and credited monthly or quarterly.
- **Consumer Impact**: Savers with funds sitting in commercial savings accounts should expect returns closely tracking the current monetary cycle, avoiding negative real returns when inflation softens.

## Digital Payment Systems & Branchless Banking Limits

| Platform / Account Tier | Daily Inward/Outward Limit | Monthly Limit | Transaction Fees | Key Regulatory Requirement |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Raast P2P (Instant Payment) | PKR 200,000 to PKR 1,000,000 (varies by bank tier) | PKR 1,000,000+ | **PKR 0 (Free)** | Linked directly to Mobile Number (Raast ID) or IBAN |
| 1BILL (Interbank Bill Payment) | Standard bank account limit | Uncapped | Free or low flat fee | Central utility/tax payment engine |
| Asaan Digital Account (ADA) | PKR 500,000 | PKR 1,000,000 total balance limit | Free opening | Minimal KYC (CNIC only, no formal income proof) |
| Asaan Remittance Account | PKR 1,000,000 balance | PKR 1,000,000 monthly remittance | Free | Dedicated to home remittance inward credits |
| Branchless Wallets (Level 1) | PKR 50,000 | PKR 200,000 | Variable cash-out fees | Biometric NADRA verification at agent or app |
| Branchless Wallets (Level 2) | PKR 100,000 | PKR 400,000 | Variable cash-out fees | Video KYC / Biometric upgrade |

## Deposit Protection Corporation (DPC) Guarantee
Deposits in all scheduled commercial banks are insured by the Deposit Protection Corporation (a wholly-owned subsidiary of SBP):
- **Protected Amount**: Up to **PKR 500,000 per depositor per bank**.
- **Eligible Accounts**: Current, savings, and fixed term deposits in domestic currency.
- **Strategy**: Individuals holding large cash reserves (e.g. over PKR 5M) are advised to distribute funds across multiple top-tier commercial banks to maximize systemic safety and DPC coverage.
