# National Savings Organization Schemes & Profit Rates (CDNS)

## Overview of CDNS
The Central Directorate of National Savings (CDNS) operates government-backed savings schemes offering sovereign-guaranteed risk-free returns. These products are popular for retirement planning, emergency liquid reserves, and monthly fixed income.

## Investment Limits & Eligibility Rules

### 1. Behbood Savings Certificates (BSC)
- **Minimum Investment**: PKR 5,000.
- **Maximum Limit**: PKR 7,500,000 (PKR 7.5 Million) for single individual; PKR 15,000,000 (PKR 15 Million) for joint eligible accounts.
- **Key Advantage**: Zero income tax deduction at source regardless of filer status. No compulsory Zakat deduction.

### 2. Regular Income Certificates (RIC)
- **Minimum Investment**: PKR 50,000.
- **Maximum Limit**: No upper limit for general public.
- **Premature Encashment Penalties**: Service charges apply if encashed within 1st, 2nd, 3rd, or 4th year (typically 1.0% to 0.25% of face value).

### 3. Sarwa Islamic Term Account (SITA)
- Shariah-compliant alternative to conventional certificates, structured on Mudarabah principle.
- Available in 1-year, 3-year, and 5-year tenures with monthly, half-yearly, or maturity profit disbursement.

## Tax and Advisory Guidelines for Personal Finance
1. **Senior Family Members**: Households can optimize returns and eliminate tax drag by gifting investment capital to eligible senior family members (parents/spouses aged 60+) to utilize the BSC quota.
2. **Filer vs Non-Filer Gap**: For RIC or standard savings certificates, non-filers lose 30% of their gross earnings to withholding tax compared to 15% for filers, significantly reducing the effective net yield.
