# FBR Income Tax Regulations & Slabs (Tax Year 2024–2025)

## Overview & Tax Year Definitions
Under the Income Tax Ordinance 2001 (as amended by Finance Act 2024), Tax Year 2025 covers the period from July 1, 2024 to June 30, 2025.
An individual is classified as "Salaried" if their salary income constitutes more than 75% of their total taxable income for the year. Otherwise, they are classified as "Non-Salaried Individual / Association of Persons (AOP)".

---

## Salaried Individual Tax Slabs (Finance Act 2024)

| Slab | Taxable Income Range (PKR) | Fixed Tax (PKR) | Variable Tax Rate on Excess |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Up to 600,000 | 0 | 0% |
| 2 | 600,001 to 1,200,000 | 0 | 5% of amount exceeding PKR 600,000 |
| 3 | 1,200,001 to 2,200,000 | 30,000 | 15% of amount exceeding PKR 1,200,000 |
| 4 | 2,200,001 to 3,200,000 | 180,000 | 25% of amount exceeding PKR 2,200,000 |
| 5 | 3,200,001 to 4,100,000 | 430,000 | 30% of amount exceeding PKR 3,200,000 |
| 6 | Exceeding 4,100,000 | 700,000 | 35% of amount exceeding PKR 4,100,000 |

### High-Income Surcharge for Salaried Individuals
For individuals with taxable income exceeding PKR 10,000,000 (10 Million PKR) per annum, a surcharge of 10% on the calculated income tax liability is applied.

## Key Advance Withholding Tax (WHT) Rates (Filer vs Non-Filer)

| Transaction Type | Active Taxpayer (Filer) Rate | Inactive / Non-Filer Rate | Notes & Legal Threshold |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bank Cash Withdrawal | 0% | 0.6% | Applies only if daily withdrawal exceeds PKR 50,000 for non-filers |
| Bank Profits (Profit on Debt) | 15% | 30% | Deducted at source on savings accounts & fixed deposits |
| National Savings Certificates | 15% | 30% | Exemptions apply to Behbood/Pensioners accounts up to limits |
| Dividend Income (Normal) | 15% | 30% | Mutual funds & listed corporate equities |
| Motor Vehicle Purchase / Registration | 1% to 3% based on CC | 3x to 5x filer rate | Substantial punitive rates for non-filers |
| Property Purchase (Advance Tax 236K) | 3% | 10.5% (up to 15%) | Based on recorded FBR fair valuation |
| Property Sale (Advance Tax 236C) | 3% | 10% | Based on holding period and filer status |

## Practical Tax Planning Tips for Pakistani Earners
1. **Active Taxpayer List (ATL) Status**: Maintaining filer status immediately cuts withholding taxes on banking, vehicle purchases, and property transactions by 50% to 70%.
2. **Voluntary Pension System (VPS)**: Contributions to SECP-registered Voluntary Pension Schemes qualify for tax credit up to 20% of taxable income under Section 63.
3. **Medical Allowance Exemption**: Reimbursed medical expenses or cash medical allowance up to 10% of basic salary is exempt from income tax if certified by employer.
